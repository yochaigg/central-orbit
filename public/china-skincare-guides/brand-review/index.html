Service 01 / US$950

Opportunity Assessment

For brands asking whether China merits a closer commercial investigation—and what they should understand before committing further resources.

01

Brand and product review

Examine the agreed products or skincare routine, core benefits, differentiation, available evidence and existing brand positioning.

02

China competitive context

Review up to five relevant products publicly listed for sale in China, including their visible claims, product sizes and indicative listed prices. Record sources and observation dates.

03

Preliminary commercial opportunity

Identify plausible customer segments, positioning opportunities and questions around price and initial consumer access. Distinguish observable evidence from hypotheses.

04

Findings and next decisions

Summarize strengths, limitations, missing information, principal risks and the three to five actions most valuable before deciding whether to progress.

Deliverable: A concise written PDF assessment with dated source references, assumptions clearly identified and one follow-up email clarification. This is an opportunity review, not a complete China launch plan.

Service 02 / US$4,500

Launch Blueprint

For brands ready to examine how a China B2C launch could be organized. The output is a decision-ready roadmap, not an offer to operate a China business on your behalf.

01

Product and customer proposition

Evaluate the selected line or routine, hero and supporting products, evidence, positioning and potential target customer segments.

02

Competitive analysis and price positioning

Review relevant Chinese-market alternatives, visible product claims, listed prices and how competing offers are presented to consumers. Identify points needing further validation.

03

B2C platform and channel recommendation

Compare suitable consumer sales routes, recommend a primary approach and credible alternative, and explain selection factors, operating considerations and dependencies.

04

Logistics and fulfillment overview

Outline relevant international shipping and domestic or bonded fulfillment considerations, warehousing, stock movement, delivery, returns and services that would require local partners or confirmation.

05

Regulatory preparation

Identify the product classification, claims, labeling and documentation issues to check for the proposed sales route, with explicit referrals for specialist verification where necessary.

06

Commercial planning framework

Map major launch and ongoing cost categories, platform and service-provider questions, and the financial inputs the brand needs to confirm. No invented profitability projections.

07

Detailed launch roadmap

Provide sequenced stages from research and preparation through channel setup requirements, launch readiness and initial performance review. Include recommended actions, suggested owners, dependencies, decision points and indicative timing.

Deliverable: A written China Skincare Launch Blueprint with documented sources, an initial channel recommendation, a logistics overview and a phased action plan. It is research-led preparation, not implementation or regulatory certification.

Scope and limitations

What the work includes—and what it does not.

Included

  • Independent desk research using identifiable public sources and supplied brand materials.
  • Commercial interpretation and recommended next actions tailored to the agreed products.
  • Clear separation of observed facts, estimates, assumptions and questions requiring verification.
  • A written deliverable for internal discussion and decision-making.

Not included

  • Consumer interviews, proprietary sales data, distributor introductions or a guarantee of market success.
  • Regulatory approvals, product registration, legal or customs advice, or certification.
  • Binding shipping quotations, supply contracts, store opening or campaign execution.
  • Ongoing operational management or implementation, unless commissioned separately.

Frequently asked questions

Before commissioning the work.

Can the review cover a skincare routine rather than only two products?

Yes. Either service can cover one coherent product line or routine, or two separate priority products. The actual included products, SKU count and level of analysis are confirmed in the written scope; multiple unrelated product ranges require a separate quotation.

What makes the Blueprint different from the Assessment?

The $950 Assessment establishes the initial commercial case and the next questions to investigate. The $4,500 Blueprint goes further into B2C platform selection, logistics models, regulatory preparation, cost considerations and a detailed launch roadmap. The Blueprint includes the underlying opportunity analysis at greater depth.

Is the assessment based on consumer interviews or actual Chinese sales figures?

No. Both standard services are based on desk research and supplied information. Public product listings and indicative prices are not evidence of actual units sold or achieved profitability. Unknowns are explicitly flagged.

Will you register our products or open an online store?

No. The work identifies relevant requirements and recommends next steps, but regulatory filings, legal advice, customs clearance, platform account opening and hands-on operations are outside these fixed-fee services.

Is confidential financial data required?

No confidential financial information is needed for the introductory conversation. If later cost assumptions would materially improve the analysis, we will identify them in the brief. You decide what information to share; missing figures are labeled as unknown rather than estimated without evidence.

When is payment due?

Only after we agree on the project scope, deliverables and terms in writing. We then provide the relevant secure Stripe link. The introductory Calendly call is free.

Can you guarantee a launch date?

No. Launch timing depends on product requirements, the selected sales route, operations and the brand's own decisions. The Blueprint provides a staged roadmap and indicative planning windows, not an unconditional launch-date promise.
