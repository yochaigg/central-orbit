01 / WHAT'S IN THE REVIEW

What are you actually paying for?

We'll take a close look at up to two products from your brand and the practical choices involved in selling them in China.

No giant presentation about China's population. No made-up market share. Just relevant, sourced findings and clearly labeled commercial recommendations.

01. The product

What are you asking consumers to buy?

We review the product benefits, claims, proof points, price positioning and brand story as an unfamiliar Chinese shopper might encounter them online.

02. The company it keeps

What would sit next to it?

We identify and compare up to five relevant skincare products publicly available in China. Not necessarily the biggest brands—the most useful comparisons for the specific category, promise and price tier.

  • Visible benefits and product claims
  • Indicative listed prices and pack sizes, with dates
  • How alternatives explain why they deserve a place in the routine
03. The money

Does the price make any sense?

We examine preliminary retail price positioning and the cost questions that affect margin: landing costs, channel fees, promotions and likely operational requirements. Any numbers requiring your private cost data will be labeled as assumptions until you provide them.

04. The route

How could the product reach buyers?

A practical comparison of plausible first channels—such as cross-border e-commerce versus general trade—highlighting the commercial and regulatory questions requiring verification. We don't certify a route or promise eligibility.

05. The decision

So what would we do next?

You'll receive the biggest positioning gaps, potential advantages, what remains unknown, and a prioritized action list. Including situations where the sensible recommendation is not to spend money launching yet.

A FEW REASONABLE QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch

Do we have to be preparing a China launch already?

No. The review can help an established brand decide whether further investigation is commercially justified. Brands already selling in China can use it to revisit positioning, product selection or channel economics.

How do you choose the competitor products?

We start with the product benefit, price tier and likely customer. We look for relevant currently listed alternatives, not simply famous names. Each comparison should include sources and observation dates; a listing does not prove sales volume.

What if you can't verify something?

We say so. Data gaps, inaccessible listings, uncertainty about actual transaction prices and questions requiring local confirmation are identified—not quietly converted into facts.

Do I have to share confidential financial information?

Not for the initial call. For a useful margin scenario you may choose to provide cost assumptions. Without them, the report will flag the missing numbers rather than invent profitability.

When do we pay?

Only after receiving and agreeing to a written scope and applicable payment terms. This page doesn't collect payment, and requesting a call does not create an obligation.

What if I need more than two products or deeper work?

That would be a separate scope and fee agreed in advance. This entry review is intentionally narrow so you know what you're buying.

What happens after I request a call?

Your email opens with a proposed subject and space for your website, time zone and preferred times. We'll arrange a slot by replying. This is an email-based scheduling request until a live booking calendar is connected.
