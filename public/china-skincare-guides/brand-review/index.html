03 / LAUNCH BLUEPRINT · $4,500

From possible opportunity to practical plan.

For brands ready to go beyond the initial question. The Blueprint lays out how to approach launch preparation, what to verify, and what decisions must come first.

It is researched planning rather than hands-on store setup, importing or day-to-day campaign management.

01. Products + consumers

Start with the right offer

A deeper review of one agreed product line, routine or two products: hero products, complementary routine purchases, benefits, evidence and potential customer segments.

02. Competing products

Know the shelf you're entering

Compare relevant products sold in China, their claims, listed prices and positioning. Separate observable evidence from assumptions; online listings alone cannot prove actual volumes or profitability.

03. Platform selection

Where should people buy it?

Evaluate suitable B2C platforms and sales routes. Recommend a primary option and a realistic alternative, with major operating requirements, trade-offs and factors to validate before committing.

04. Logistics overview

How would orders reach consumers?

Compare direct shipping, warehousing or fulfillment approaches where applicable. Cover indicative processes, inventory flow, delivery, returns, and which local partners or eligibility requirements would need confirmation. Not freight quotes or customs clearance services.

05. Regulatory preparation

What must be checked before a sale?

Map the product classification, documentation, claims and labeling questions that may affect the proposed route. Note what requires qualified regulatory and customs verification; no certification or filing is included.

06. Detailed launch roadmap

What happens first, second and third?

A phased, brand-specific plan covering preparation, channel and service-provider decisions, launch execution prerequisites, and post-launch review. Each stage includes actions, dependencies, suggested responsibilities, decision points and indicative timing.

07. Commercial planning

Know what still needs budgeting

Outline major investment and operating-cost categories and list the figures the brand needs to obtain or validate. This is an assumptions framework, not a binding budget or guaranteed return.

QUESTIONS BEFORE BOOKING

A few things worth knowing.

Can you evaluate a skincare routine instead of just two products?

Yes. We can cover two individual products or one coherent line or skincare routine—for example, a cleanser, serum and moisturizer designed to work together. We will agree on the actual included products and analysis depth in writing before any payment. Multiple unrelated ranges are outside the standard scope.

What's the difference between the $950 Assessment and the $4,500 Blueprint?

The Assessment examines whether further China investigation looks worthwhile and identifies the main open questions. The Blueprint includes deeper market work plus recommended B2C channels, platform comparisons, a logistics overview, regulatory preparation questions, launch cost categories and a detailed staged roadmap.

How do you select competitors?

We start with the product category, benefits, likely customer and intended price positioning. We identify relevant products that are publicly visible for sale in China, note when and where they were listed, and explain important evidence gaps.

What if something cannot be confirmed?

We identify the unknown and explain what further verification is required. We do not turn indicative online prices, public listings or unverified assumptions into a promise of sales or profitability.

Do we have to share confidential financial information?

No sensitive information is required for the initial conversation. Where deeper cost assumptions are relevant, you can decide what to share securely after the scope is agreed; unknown values will be labeled as such.

Can you guarantee a six-month launch?

No. Time depends on the products, selected route, regulatory and logistical requirements and the client's resources. The Blueprint provides a researched sequence and indicative planning windows rather than an unconditional launch date.

When do we pay, and can we book without paying?

The 20-minute Calendly call is free. After that, any project proceeds only when the written scope, fee, delivery expectations and payment terms have been agreed. If you choose to proceed, use the corresponding secure Stripe payment link above. Clicking a payment button opens Stripe in a new tab.
